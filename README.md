### // Engineering Profile

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineer focused on bridging hardware architecture and applied signal processing. My work involves designing custom RISC-V processors, developing digital hardware accelerators, and simulating complex RF and control systems. I am passionate about understanding systems from the logic gate level up to the algorithmic layer, and I continuously explore new methodologies in digital design, embedded development, and engineering simulation.

### // Core Competencies

* **Digital Hardware & Architecture:** RTL Design, FPGA Prototyping, Computer Architecture (RISC-V)
* **Applied Engineering:** Digital Signal Processing (DSP), RF / Antenna Systems, Control Systems
* **Simulation & Analysis:** Mathematical Modeling, System Simulation, Electromagnetic Analysis

### // Selected Engineering Work

Here is a curated selection of repositories demonstrating my approach across different engineering domains. These projects serve as examples of my technical workspace and current areas of exploration.

#### [5-Stage Pipelined RISC-V Processor (RV32I)](https://github.com/prahas12/5-Stage-Pipelined-RISC-V-Processor-RV32I)
> **Domain:** Computer Architecture | **Technologies:** SystemVerilog<br>

> **Domain:** Digital Hardware | **Technologies:** Verilog, FPGA<br>

> **Domain:** RF / Signal Processing | **Technologies:** MATLAB<br>
